# ZED - Resource Management Systems

## Overview
Resource management in ZED creates meaningful tactical decisions by forcing players to balance aggressive clearing strategies against conservative resource preservation. Every bullet, medical supply, and piece of equipment represents a calculated risk in the post-apocalyptic extraction environment.

## Core Resources
### 1. Ammunition
**Resource Type**: Consumable, mission-critical  
**Scarcity Level**: High - limited carrying capacity and availability  
**Tactical Impact**:  
- Ammunition comes in various types (FMJ, HP, AP, specialty) each offering distinct tactical advantages—choose and conserve based on mission needs, target type, and resource scarcity.
- Forces weapon choice decisions (pistol vs rifle vs shotgun)
- Encourages precision shooting over spray-and-pray tactics
- Creates tension between thorough clearing and ammo conservation
- Drives exploration for ammo pickups in dangerous areas

**Resource Sources**:
- Zombie drops (random chance)
- Hidden caches in buildings
- Thematic loot placement: resource types are more likely in logical locations (e.g., medical supplies in bathrooms, cupboards, and hospital rooms; food in kitchens; advanced meds in central doctor’s offices or medical storage units)
- Base crafting (requires materials and time)
- Mission rewards for successful extraction

### 2. Health and Medical Supplies
**Resource Type**: Consumable, survival-critical
**Scarcity Level**: Medium - available but requires strategic use
**Tactical Impact**:
- Injuries affect movement speed and accuracy
- Medical supplies have different healing rates and effects
- Forces risk assessment: push through injury or use precious meds
- Creates tactical positioning priorities (avoid damage vs aggressive clearing)

**Medical Supply Types**:
- **Bandages**: Stop bleeding, basic healing over time
- **Painkillers**: Temporary performance boost, mask injury effects
- **Antibiotics**: Prevent infection from untreated wounds
- **Surgical Kits**: Full healing but rare and time-consuming to use

### 3. Equipment Durability
**Resource Type**: Degrading, maintenance-required
**Scarcity Level**: Medium - manageable with proper planning
**Tactical Impact**:
- Weapons jam or break during critical moments
- Armor loses effectiveness as it degrades
- Forces equipment rotation and backup planning
- Creates tension between using best gear vs preserving it

**Degradation Factors**:
- Combat usage (firing weapons, taking hits)
- Environmental conditions (moisture, debris)
- Time in field (extended missions cause wear)
- Maintenance availability (base workshop upgrades)

### 4. Mission Time
**Resource Type**: Invisible pressure, escalating threat
**Scarcity Level**: High - constantly depleting
**Tactical Impact**:
- Longer missions increase zombie spawn rates
- Noise attracts more threats over time
- Extraction windows may close or become more dangerous
- Forces speed vs thoroughness decisions

**Time Pressure Mechanics**:
- **Early Phase**: Sparse enemies, quiet exploration
- **Mid Phase**: Increased patrols, noise-based spawning
- **Late Phase**: Swarm behavior, multiple threat types
- **Critical Phase**: Overwhelming odds, emergency extraction only

## Tactical Decision Framework

### Conservative Strategy
**Resource Philosophy**: Preserve resources for future missions
**Tactical Approach**:
- Minimal engagement, stealth when possible
- Early extraction with partial loot
- Melee weapons to conserve ammo
- Avoid high-risk areas with better loot

**Advantages**:
- Consistent mission success rate
- Steady resource accumulation
- Lower medical costs
- Reduced equipment wear

**Disadvantages**:
- Slower progression
- Limited access to rare materials
- Missed opportunities for valuable loot
- Longer time to unlock advanced areas

### Aggressive Strategy
**Resource Philosophy**: Risk resources for maximum gain
**Tactical Approach**:
- Full building clearance
- Engage all threats for maximum loot
- Use best equipment and ammunition
- Push into dangerous high-value areas

**Advantages**:
- Rapid progression and base development
- Access to rare and valuable resources
- Faster unlock of advanced equipment
- Higher mission rewards

**Disadvantages**:
- High failure rate and resource loss
- Expensive medical and repair costs
- Equipment degradation
- Risk of total team loss in Permadeath mode

### Balanced Strategy
**Resource Philosophy**: Calculated risks based on mission context
**Tactical Approach**:
- Selective engagement based on threat assessment
- Partial clearing with strategic extraction timing
- Equipment rotation to manage durability
- Risk escalation based on current resource levels

## Permadeath Consequences

### Standard Difficulty
**Character Death**: 
- Lose all carried equipment and loot
- Medical costs for recovery (25-50% of base medical supplies)
- Injury effects lasting 1-2 missions
- Morale impact on other team members

**Mission Failure**:
- All mission progress lost
- Preparation resources wasted (ammo, medical supplies used)
- Time investment lost
- Potential equipment degradation from failed extraction

### Permadeath Difficulty
**Character Death**:
- Permanent loss of character and all carried items
- Specialized skills and experience lost
- Team composition disrupted
- Potential campaign failure if no survivors remain

**Survivor Impact**:
- Morale penalties affecting performance
- Increased medical costs for trauma treatment
- Reduced mission effectiveness until replacement recruited
- Strategic reassessment of team capabilities

## Mission Structure and Resource Flow

### Pre-Mission Phase
**Resource Allocation**:
1. **Equipment Selection**: Choose weapons, armor, medical supplies
2. **Ammo Distribution**: Allocate limited ammunition across team
3. **Role Assignment**: Designate point man, support, medic roles
4. **Backup Planning**: Prepare emergency extraction protocols

**Strategic Considerations**:
- Mission difficulty vs team capability
- Resource investment vs potential rewards
- Equipment durability before deployment
- Medical readiness of team members

### Mission Execution Phase
**Resource Management During Combat**:

#### Room Clearing
- **Ammunition Conservation**: Precise shots vs suppressive fire
- **Health Management**: When to use medical supplies vs push through
- **Equipment Preservation**: Rotate damaged gear, avoid unnecessary risks
- **Time Management**: Speed vs thoroughness in each room

#### Threat Escalation
- **Early Rooms**: Conservative ammo use, melee when possible
- **Mid Rooms**: Balanced approach, tactical positioning
- **Late Rooms**: Aggressive clearing, use best equipment
- **Boss Encounters**: All resources committed, survival priority

#### Loot Collection
- **Carry Capacity**: Weight vs value decisions
- **Risk Assessment**: Dangerous areas vs reward potential
- **Time Pressure**: Quick grab vs thorough search
- **Team Coordination**: Who carries what based on survival priority

### Post-Mission Phase
**Resource Processing**:
1. **Medical Treatment**: Heal injuries, prevent infection
2. **Equipment Maintenance**: Repair degraded gear
3. **Resource Allocation**: Store vs immediate use decisions
4. **Base Development**: Invest in upgrades vs stockpile

## Resource Scarcity and Tactical Positioning

### Ammunition Scarcity Effects
**High Ammo Situations**:
- Aggressive positioning, direct engagement
- Room-to-room clearing without stealth
- Suppressive fire to control zombie movement
- Confidence in extended firefights

**Low Ammo Situations**:
- Defensive positioning, chokepoint control
- Stealth and avoidance when possible
- Melee weapon reliance
- Strategic retreat and early extraction

### Medical Supply Scarcity Effects
**Well-Stocked Medical**:
- Aggressive positioning, acceptable risk-taking
- Point man tactics, leading from front
- Willingness to take damage for better positioning
- Extended mission duration

**Limited Medical**:
- Defensive positioning, cover prioritization
- Avoid unnecessary risks and damage
- Conservative engagement ranges
- Early extraction when injured

### Equipment Durability Effects
**Fresh Equipment**:
- Confident in gear performance
- Aggressive tactics using best weapons
- Extended mission capability
- Willingness to engage tough enemies

**Degraded Equipment**:
- Conservative tactics, backup planning
- Avoid situations requiring peak performance
- Prioritize equipment preservation
- Earlier extraction to prevent total failure

## Integration with Base Development

### Resource Investment Priorities
1. **Medical Bay**: Reduces post-mission recovery costs
2. **Workshop**: Enables equipment maintenance and crafting
3. **Storage**: Increases resource carrying capacity
4. **Communications**: Improves mission intel and planning

### Progression Gates
- **Advanced Missions**: Require minimum resource stockpiles
- **Equipment Unlocks**: Need specific rare materials
- **Team Expansion**: Requires medical facilities for new recruits
- **Facility Upgrades**: Demand significant resource investment

## Success Metrics and Resource Efficiency

### Mission Success Indicators
- **Resource Positive**: Gained more than invested
- **Break Even**: Maintained current resource levels
- **Acceptable Loss**: Strategic investment for progression
- **Critical Failure**: Unsustainable resource drain

### Long-term Sustainability
- **Resource Generation Rate**: Base production vs consumption
- **Mission Success Rate**: Consistency of positive outcomes
- **Team Capability Growth**: Improved efficiency over time
- **Strategic Flexibility**: Ability to adapt to different mission types

*This resource management system creates meaningful tactical decisions by making every bullet, bandage, and piece of equipment a strategic consideration that directly impacts mission success and long-term campaign viability.*
